Line To Spot

Connect matching colors without crossing lines.

Line To Spot is a simple and intuitive puzzle game where players draw lines to connect matching colors without crossing other paths.

The game offers relaxing gameplay, multiple solutions, and over 300 levels.

Developed by Jmath Studio for mobile puzzle game players on iOS and Android.

Features

  • Simple and intuitive controls
  • Multiple solutions for each puzzle
  • Relaxing puzzle gameplay
  • Over 300 levels
