Frequently asked questions

How do you play Line To Spot?

Draw lines to connect pairs of dots of the same color. The lines cannot cross. Connect every pair to solve the puzzle.

Is there a time limit?

No. You can work through each puzzle at your own pace, without a time limit.

How many levels are there?

Line To Spot has over 300 levels.

Is Line To Spot available on iOS and Android?

Yes. You can download Line To Spot from the App Store or Google Play.

Which languages does the game support?

Line To Spot supports English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, and Indonesian.
