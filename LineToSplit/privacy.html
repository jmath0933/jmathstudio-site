Privacy Policy

Jmath Studio operates the mobile puzzle game "Line To Split" (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

Certain information may be collected automatically by third-party services used by the Game.

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, ADID)
  • IP address
  • Basic usage and diagnostic information

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect and process information in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

For more information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Local Storage

Game progress, settings, and gameplay data are stored locally on your device.

Jmath Studio does not collect or transmit this information to external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services
  • Apple App Store Services

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Game and its service providers.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
